# One Line, Big Laugh

Entry for the [One Line, Big Laugh](https://make.fun/bounty/u-coral-404-one-line-big-laugh-328fb857) competition on make.fun.

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only, deterministic, no network) writes one funny
line to `/output/entry.txt`:

> My dog learned to open the fridge, so I hid the leftovers in the oven — where he found the lasagna I'd hidden from myself in 2019.

The narrator outsmarts his own dog by hiding the leftovers in the oven —
and the twist lands in the last eight words, when the dog finds the lasagna
the narrator had hidden from himself seven years ago. A self-own: the hiding
spot was so good even its owner forgot it.
